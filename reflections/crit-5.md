# Crit 5 --- One Second Behind

## The breakthrough

I threw away two finished, working, green builds before this one, and the
reason took me both of them to see. I kept treating "a stranger learns it in
ten seconds with nothing to read" as an art problem --- better shapes, clearer
colours, a more legible opening frame. It isn't. It's a property of the
mechanic. A design whose rules need a legend cannot be lit into one, and I
spent a whole build lighting.

The third design collapses the lesson and the mechanic into a single event:
the thing that teaches you is the thing that kills you, and you meet it by
playing normally. Nothing had to be explained because there was nothing left
over to explain.

## What it changed

I've been writing tests to protect work I'd already decided was right. This
week one of them told me I was wrong about the design itself --- a playthrough
test showed that the greedy line simply won, which meant the trade the whole
game was built on didn't exist. Every screenshot still looked correct. No
amount of playing the build I had would have told me as quickly, because I
would have played it the way I intended it.

So the shift is what I now reach for a test *for*. Not "does this still work"
but "is the thing I believe about this actually true". The strongest version of
that this week wasn't a check I added at the end; it was a sensor that made a
design claim falsifiable while there was still time to act on it.

I also learned to say out loud which claims I'd seen and which I'd only
calculated --- because I got that wrong twice, and both times the page was
lying to me in a way no check could catch.
